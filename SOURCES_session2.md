# Data provenance

Rule 8 of Rule et al. (2019): data are shared with their description, origin and date. This file
is also the model of the provenance students record in exercise 7 of Lab 2.1.

## Current state: synthetic data

Everything in `data/` is generated by `scripts/make_data.py` with a fixed seed (`20260925`).
Structure, encoding and defects reproduce the originals; **no project, laboratory, reservoir or
bulletin shown here exists.**

| File | What it is | Deliberate defects |
|---|---|---|
| `anejo_geotecnico.pdf` | Geology and geotechnics annex, 4 pages, 63 SPT tests in 6 boreholes | Table split across pages 2 and 3 with the header repeated; decimal comma; units only in the header |
| `certificado_hormigon_escaneado.pdf` | Concrete compression test certificate, 12 specimens, rasterised at 300 dpi | No text layer; 0.6° skew; gaussian noise and specks; reproducible OCR errors on isolated digits and decimal commas |

## Replacing with real documents

**Geotechnical annex.** Public-information portals of road administrations publish complete
projects with annexes. Choose one whose introduction declares the number of boreholes and tests:
that statement is the control figure of exercise 5. Check the publication's deadline and purpose
before redistributing.

**Test certificate.** Any accredited laboratory certificate, anonymised, scanned at 300 dpi.

**Monitoring series.** A real datalogger export with its manufacturer header intact; anonymise
the equipment and site identifiers.

**Hydrological bulletin.** Basin authorities publish weekly bulletins with storage by reservoir.
Save the complete page and note the download date.

**Sentinel-2 scene.** Copernicus Data Space Ecosystem, free account. Download the Level-2A
product of two contrasting dates over the same reservoir and clip to the area of interest before
committing: a full product is about 1 GB. Keep the metadata file, which exercise 4 reads. Open
licence with attribution to Copernicus.

When replacing: record URL, download date and licence here; re-run `make_data.py --comprobar`
or compute the expected values by hand and update the asserts and the README table; regenerate
the figures; run both solution notebooks end to end; freeze a copy in the repository.
